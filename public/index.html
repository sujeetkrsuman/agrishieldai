📝 Pathology Description

Late blight is a destructive disease of tomato crops caused by the oomycete Phytophthora infestans. It thrives in wet, cool conditions, causing dark green to black water-soaked lesions on leaves and large brown firm lesions on fruits.

🍃 Organic Control Methods
  • Prune infected stems immediately.
  • Apply copper fungicide spray.
🧪 Chemical Remedies
  • Apply Mancozeb or Chlorothalonil.

🛡️ Preventive Practices

  • Use drip irrigation.
  • Crop rotation of 3 years.
